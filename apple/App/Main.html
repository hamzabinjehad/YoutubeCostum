Turn it on

  1. 1Open Settings.
  2. 2Tap Apps, then Safari, then Extensions.
  3. 3Turn on Costum, then allow it on youtube.com.

Once it’s on

A round button appears at the corner of any YouTube page. Tap it for speed, sound and picture controls, plus switches for whatever the page you are on is cluttered with.

Everything is set on this device and synced through your own iCloud account. There is no account to make and no server in between.
